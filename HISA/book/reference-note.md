# The Book's Reference Note

The following short passage is offered for the book to point readers here. It would sit
in the chapter "How Biology, Experience, and Choice Shape a Person", which treats the
word "programming" as an analogy **[B]**. The current edition of the book does not yet
include it.

---

> **A note on the open specification.** This chapter compares a person with a computing
> machine and marks the comparison as an analogy [B]. A companion repository works the
> comparison out in full, in the style of the manuals of real processors such as the DEC
> PDP-11 and RISC-V. It lists a base instruction set shared by living organisms, the human
> extensions (language, reasoning, self-reference, volition, time-binding, affect, and
> the proposed spiritual channel), a vector extension for the brain's parallel
> processing, and a neural-network extension explaining why most human capabilities are
> learned rather than built in. You are invited to read it, question it, and contribute.
>
> Repository: **https://github.com/weqaar/scienceofunseen/tree/main/HISA**
>
> The specification is deliberately unfinished. Like this book, it labels established
> sources and findings **[A]**, explanatory models and analogies **[B]**, and unverified
> hypotheses **[C]**. A person remains more than a machine executing instructions, and the
> specification describes only the machinery.

---
