# The Book's Reference Note

The following short passage is what the book uses to point readers here. It is written to
drop into the "Human Programmability" chapter, near the "Building HISA in the open"
subsection.

---

> **A note on the open specification.** Because HISA is a *specification* - a description
> of operations and how they compose - it can be written down, versioned, and refined the
> way engineers maintain a real instruction set. An evolving draft is published openly as
> a companion to this book, modelled on the manuals of real architectures such as the
> DEC PDP-11 and RISC-V. It lists the base living-organism instruction set (LISA), the
> human standard extensions (language, reasoning, self-reference, volition, time-binding,
> affect, and the proposed spiritual channel), a vector extension describing the human as
> a massively parallel machine beyond any silicon design, and the microarchitecture,
> compiler, and operating-system layers. Readers who wish to analyse, critique, or extend
> the model - or simply to see the argument of this chapter stated in full engineering
> detail - are invited to read and contribute.
>
> Repository: **https://github.com/&lt;your-handle&gt;/HISA**  *(replace with the real URL
> once published)*
>
> The specification is deliberately unfinished, and marks throughout what is established
> **[A]**, what is a defensible model **[B]**, and what is speculative **[C]** - the same
> discipline this book keeps.

---
