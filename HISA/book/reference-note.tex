\begin{keybox}
\keyboxtitle{A note on the open specification}
This chapter compares a person with a computing machine and marks the comparison as an
analogy \textbf{[B]}. A companion repository works the comparison out in full, in the
style of the manuals of real processors such as the DEC PDP-11 and RISC-V. It lists a
base instruction set shared by living organisms, the human extensions (language,
reasoning, self-reference, volition, time-binding, affect, and the proposed spiritual
channel), a vector extension for the brain's parallel processing, and a neural-network
extension explaining why most human capabilities are learned rather than built in. You
are invited to read it, question it, and contribute.

\medskip
\noindent Repository: \href{https://github.com/weqaar/scienceofunseen/tree/main/HISA}{\texttt{github.com/weqaar/scienceofunseen}}

\medskip
\noindent The specification is deliberately unfinished. Like this book, it labels
established sources and findings \textbf{[A]}, explanatory models and analogies
\textbf{[B]}, and unverified hypotheses \textbf{[C]}. A person remains more than a
machine executing instructions, and the specification describes only the machinery.
\end{keybox}
